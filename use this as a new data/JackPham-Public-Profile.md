## About (LinkedIn About, portfolio intro)

I am an Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences student at UC Berkeley (graduating May 2028) who likes the place where circuits meet data: measuring real hardware, modeling it, and building tools that make the measurements easier to trust.

Right now I am a Product Engineer Co-Op at Skyworks Solutions in Irvine, where I characterize RF power amplifiers and low-noise amplifiers during Final Test, analyze yield with Cpk, SPC and ANOVA, and build Python, C++ and Rust pipelines for production test data. Outside work I volunteer as electrical engineering support for a community-led solar, battery storage and EV charging microgrid in Huron, CA, alongside graduate students at UCLA and USF.

My projects cover high-speed link design (a SerDes model and RTL verified with cocotb, plus a KiCad test board), embedded machine learning on an ESP32-S3, and a low-cost household methane sensor. I started at Cypress College, where I earned an associate degree in mathematics and physics before transferring to Berkeley.

I am looking for Summer 2027 internships and research opportunities in IC design, RF and analog, digital design and verification, and hardware test.
